## 1. Reference Architecture & Benchmark Study

The goal of this benchmark study is to identify best-in-class interaction patterns from established, privacy-respecting open-source applications and official platform design guidelines. PeerLink adapts verified patterns without copying proprietary assets or compromising its own zero-trust P2P protocol semantics.

## 3. Synthesis: The PeerLink Unified Experience

By synthesizing the best interaction patterns from these reference products while enforcing PeerLink's non-negotiable identity (privacy-first, zero-trust, causal vectors, and no cloud files):
1. **Share Center** achieves the clean simplicity of LocalSend without sacrificing PeerLink's out-of-band cryptographic trust.
2. **Media Gallery** provides the fluid chronological browsing and non-destructive editing of Fossify Gallery.
3. **Player Hub** delivers the professional media playback controls of AndroidX Media3 and Voice.
4. **File Manager** gains the clear breadcrumb navigation and conflict safety of Material Files.
5. **Document Studio** implements the seamless 3-step capture-to-PDF workflow of Google ML Kit.
6. **Sync & Backup** communicates convergent replication vs Merkle snapshot recovery with the conceptual rigor of Syncthing and BorgBackup.
